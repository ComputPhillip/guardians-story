# Guardians: Story Bible

The story, lore and narrative design for **Guardians**. The game's design files and code use the working title *Stewards*.

The story is built on the world defined in `tradable_economy_v69.xlsx`, `stewards_combat_system.md` and `Stewards_Living_World_Map.html`. Every town, faction, region, landmark, rank and ability named here comes from those files, so the story and the systems describe the same world.

**Tone:** *Game of Thrones* political drama in a grounded, low-magic medieval world. Lords scheme over taxes and titles while something old and hungry wakes in the Blight. Steel decides most fights; magic is rare, costly and simple: a bolt of fire, a ward of light, a ring of binding sigils.

## Canon rules

1. **The design files come first.** Names, places, numbers and ranks follow the workbook and the Living World Map, including the map's lords, heroes and named incidents. If they change, update the story to match.
2. **No high fantasy.** There are no dragons on the wing, no teleporting and no resurrection. Death is permanent; a beaten fighter is wounded, not slain.
3. **Every power has a source:** steel and training, faith, arcane study, alchemy or the Blight.
4. **People want things.** Every lord, guild master and outlaw chief has a goal, a fear and a price.
