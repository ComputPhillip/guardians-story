# 2. Houses and Factions

Names in this file follow the **Living World Map** rosters. The map already gives every keep a lord, a lady, a steward, two priests, three heroes, a squire and two sergeants. The story adds personalities, goals and family members, which are marked *(story)*.

Three lords are named John, so the story tells them apart with epithets: **the Coin** (Sunward), **Greybeard** (Canopy Rest) and **Ironhand** (Stonepass).

## How a hold is ruled

* **The lord and lady** (*The keep*, level cap 60) hunt, hear petitioners and hire sellswords. The map runs a lord's hunt on most days, and those hunts are where the politics happen.
* **The steward** runs the household, the granary and the books. This office is all that's left of the old Stewards order (see [01-world.md](01-world.md)). In **Canopy Rest** and **Reedwatch** the lord still sits in a **Stewards Hall** instead of a keep. These are old order buildings, and petitioners still come to them for judgement.
* **The lord's three heroes** (*Heroes*, level cap 100) are the hold's champions. They lodge at the **hero lodge**. When an outlaw camp near the town grows past 30 men, the lord sends a hero to break it. A player can become one of these heroes (the Hero path).
* **The garrison** (25 men at arms with two sergeants) escorts the weekly grain carts from the hamlets and patrols as far as the town's food allows. A Thriving town patrols far; a Starving one only mans its gate.

## The adventurers' guilds

Each hold has a guild that sends parties of five from its tavern. Guild members are the **Adventurers** group (level cap 80). A guild has about 30 members in quiet times and up to 90 when hamlets are being raided. Recruits arrive at one a day to replace the dead.

| Guild tavern | Hold | Faces | Guildmaster *(story)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riftgate | First Hearth | The Broken March | **Odo Pell**, a former Steward and the player's first mentor |
| Westwatch | Canopy Rest | Frontier | **Sela Thorn**, a Hunter |
| Oxdon | Reedwatch | Frontier | **"Mother" Agna**, who sells contracts to anyone |
| Blightwatch | Sunward Exchange | Blighted | **Vashti Kell**, Dame Matilda's agent |
| Cairn Gate | Stonepass Refuge | The Broken March | **Old Torbin**, who still keeps the Steward oath |

Other institutions also send out expeditions of five: **the Hunters' Lodge** (beast dens), **the Arcane Academy** (relics from ruins) and **the Temple** (cleansing barrows of the restless dead).

## Drakrevor's horde: the Demon King

* **Drakrevor, the Cataclysm of the Heart**, is what the common folk call **the Demon King**. It is the only creature in the world with a day of its own: it slumbers, broods, gathers power, and then dominates and ravages. **The Heart surges at night.**
* **Its holdings:** 16 rifts, 22 blight nests, 7 broken forts and 4 calamity lairs, served by demons, Horrors, Beasts and Vermin.
* **Rifts** are dormant or **surging**, and the surging ones in the Riftlands and the Blighted are where the horde spills out.
* **Blight leaks:** if no guild party visits a Blighted lair for three weeks, a pack walks out on the nearest hamlet. This is the system pressure behind the whole story.
* **Three free calamities** in the north Blighted are rivals to Drakrevor, *neutral* to the horde, and each "is named when it rises". A named calamity, once killed, never comes back.

The wild (10 dens, 5 hound runs, the barrows and the deserted farm) belongs to no one.
