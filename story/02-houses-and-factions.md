# 2. Houses and Factions

Every person in this file is a real person from the **Living World Map** rosters. The map gives every keep a lord, a lady, a custodian, two priests, three heroes, a squire and two sergeants, and the towns have their own mages, monks, guild veterans and wanderers. The story adds only personalities and goals; the lords' households have no children, so no one in the story is an heir.

Three lords are named John, so the story tells them apart with epithets: **the Coin** (Sol-Spire), **Greybeard** (Ribcage Vault) and **Ironhand** (Basalt Redoubt).

## How a hold is ruled

* **The lord and lady** (*The keep*, level cap 60) hunt, hear petitioners and hire sellswords. The map runs a lord's hunt on most days, and those hunts are where the politics happen.
* **The custodian** runs the household, the granary and the books. This office is all that's left of the old Custodians order (see [01-world.md](01-world.md)). In **Ribcage Vault** and **Aquifer Gate** the lord still sits in a **Custodians Hall** instead of a keep. These are old order buildings, and petitioners still come to them for judgement.
* **The lord's three heroes** (*Heroes*, level cap 100) are the hold's champions. They lodge at the **hero lodge**. When an outlaw camp near the town grows past 30 men, the lord sends a hero to break it. A player can become one of these heroes (the Hero path).
* **The garrison** (25 men at arms with two sergeants) escorts the weekly grain carts from the hamlets and patrols as far as the town's food allows. A Thriving town patrols far; a Starving one only mans its gate.

## The adventurers' guilds

Each hold has a guild that sends parties of five from its tavern. Guild members are the **Adventurers** group (level cap 80). A guild has about 30 members in quiet times and up to 90 when hamlets are being raided. Recruits arrive at one a day to replace the dead.

| Guild tavern | Hold | Faces | Guild leader (the highest-level member on the map) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fracture-Gate | First Monolith | The Fracture Verge | **John Thomson**, a former Custodian and the player's first mentor |
| Occident-Needle | Ribcage Vault | Frontier | **Richard Thomson**, a Hunter |
| Oxid-Plinth | Aquifer Gate | Frontier | **Juliana Carter**, who sells contracts to anyone |
| Necro-Needle | Sol-Spire Concourse | Necro-Plinth | **Beatrice Black**, Dame Matilda's agent |
| Cairn Gate | Basalt Redoubt | The Fracture Verge | **Isabella Robson**, who still keeps the Custodian oath |

Other institutions also send out expeditions of five: **the Hunters' Lodge** (beast dens), **the Arcane Academy** (relics from ruins) and **the Temple** (cleansing barrows of the restless dead).

## Drakrevor's horde: the Demon King

* **Drakrevor, the Cataclysm of the Heart**, is what the common folk call **the Demon King**. It is the only creature in the world with a day of its own: it slumbers, broods, gathers power, and then dominates and ravages. **The Heart surges at night.**
* **Its holdings:** 16 rifts, 22 blight nests, 7 broken forts and 4 calamity lairs, served by demons, Horrors, Beasts and Vermin.
* **Rifts** are dormant or **surging**, and the surging ones in the Fracture Shelf and the Necro-Plinth are where the horde spills out.
* **Blight leaks:** if no guild party visits a Necro-Plinth lair for three weeks, a pack walks out on the nearest hamlet. This is the system pressure behind the whole story.
* **Three free calamities** in the north Necro-Plinth are rivals to Drakrevor, *neutral* to the horde, and each "is named when it rises". A named calamity, once killed, never comes back.

The wild (10 dens, 5 hound runs, the barrows and the deserted farm) belongs to no one.
