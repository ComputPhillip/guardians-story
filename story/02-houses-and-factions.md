# 2. Houses and Factions

## The great houses

Each hold is ruled from its **keep** by a lord and household (NPC group *The keep*, level cap 60). The houses below are proposals; rename them freely.

### House Hale of First Hearth
*Sigil: a golden sheaf on brown. Words: "We Feed the Realm."*

* **Lord Edric Hale.** A cousin of the dead king and the strongest claimant to the throne. He is patient, generous in public and ruthless in private. He wants the crown and will let another hold burn to get it.
* **Lady Maren Hale.** His wife and the real strategist. She runs the Riftgate guild contracts through intermediaries.
* **Ser Corwin Hale.** His bastard half-brother, a former Steward and Edric's sword. He is the most dangerous duellist in the realm and is quietly ashamed of what he does.

### House Varrow of Canopy Rest
*Sigil: a white stag beneath an oak. Words: "Slow Roots Hold."*

* **Lady Ysolde Varrow.** A widowed regent, devout and cautious. Her family's chapel holds the last intact copy of the Stewards' Oath.
* **Brother Tamsin.** Her confessor, a Faith healer and a secret Blight-cultist (see Act III).
* **Aelis Varrow.** Ysolde's daughter, aged 17. She has an *Arcane spark* talent and is a potential companion.

### House Corran of Reedwatch
*Sigil: a heron on grey water. Words: "We Endure."*

* **Lord Bram Corran.** An old, half-blind lord with no money. He is losing his hold to the outlaw bands and knows it. He is honest, the rarest thing in the realm, and powerless.
* **Wenna Corran.** His granddaughter and heir. She is a fen-raised Ranger, blunt and fierce, and a companion candidate.

### House Sorel of Sunward Exchange
*Sigil: a gold coin on a red sun. Words: "Every Road Leads Here."*

* **Prince-Merchant Casimir Sorel.** He is not a lord by blood; he bought the title. He is charming and the richest man alive. He secretly pays four outlaw bands to raid his rivals' caravans.
* **Idris Sorel.** His sister, a Court Mage. She knows the Blighted better than anyone because Sunward's tavern, *Blightwatch*, faces it, and she has been selling Blight-salt to alchemists.

### House Durn of Stonepass Refuge
*Sigil: a black hammer on a grey cairn. Words: "Stone Remembers."*

* **Lady Hrothild Durn.** The war-chief of the highland clans, a Hammer Breaker and a warrior lord. She hates First Hearth's claim and would rather the crown stayed empty forever.
* **Gerrit Durn.** Her son, a Spear Guardsman. He is honourable and in love with the wrong house's daughter (Aelis Varrow).

## The adventurers' guilds

Every hold has a guild that sends out parties of five from its tavern. Guild members are the **Adventurers** group (level cap 80). The guilds are officially neutral, but in practice each lord pays for contracts that suit them.

| Guild tavern | Hold | Faces | Guildmaster (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riftgate | First Hearth | The Broken March | **Odo Pell**, a former Steward and the player's first mentor |
| Westwatch | Canopy Rest | Frontier | **Sela Thorn**, a Hunter who trusts beasts more than lords |
| Oxdon | Reedwatch | Frontier | **"Mother" Agna**, who sells contracts to whoever pays, outlaws included |
| Blightwatch | Sunward Exchange | Blighted | **Vashti Kell**, Idris Sorel's agent |
| Cairn Gate | Stonepass Refuge | The Broken March | **Old Torbin**, a Guardsman who still keeps the Steward oath |

## The outlaw bands

These come from the workbook's Factions sheet. Bands **feud** when their reaches overlap, and the winner absorbs the loser's men. All of them are at **war** with the horde.

| Band | Men | Camp | Story hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grey Crows** | 38 | Three camps east of Reedwatch | The largest band. Its chief, **Harlan Grey**, wants to become a lord by seizing Reedwatch. He is the realm's would-be *usurper from below*. |
| **Red Crows** | 13 | North-east of Reedwatch | A splinter of the Grey Crows that hates Harlan. They can be turned into allies. |
| **Iron Reavers** | 29 | West of Stonepass | Deserters from House Durn's garrison who are secretly paid by House Hale to raid Stonepass ore wagons. |
| **Salt Jackals** | 12 | North-east of Sunward | Sorel's hired raiders. They hide their loot on **Saltcrown Mesa**. |
| **Ash Hounds** | 13 | East of Sunward | Blight-scarred survivors of a fallen fort. They hunt Horrors for coin. |
| **Dust Riders** | 10 | East of Sunward | Desert horse-thieves who know the safe routes around the Blighted. |
| **Thornback Brothers** | 11 | South-east of First Hearth | Poachers in the king's old deer park. Folk heroes to the peasants. |
| **Blackwater Men** | 14 | South-east of Reedwatch | Fen smugglers who run the coves at the sea cliffs. |
| **Hollow Knives** | 14 | Far east of Reedwatch | Assassins for hire. Every house has used them at least once. |

## Drakrevor's horde

* **Drakrevor, the Cataclysm of the Heart** (tier 5): the sleeping power at the centre of the Blighted.
* **Its holdings:** 16 rifts, 22 blight nests, 7 broken forts and 4 calamity lairs.
* **Its servants:** Vermin, Beasts and **Horrors** (tier 3, numbering in the thousands), and the **Calamities** near the Heart.
* **Three free calamities** in the north Blighted are not Drakrevor's. They are rivals, *neutral* to the horde, and each "is named when it rises". They are the story's wildcards (see Act IV).

The wild (10 dens, 5 hound runs, the barrow and the deserted farm) belongs to no one.
