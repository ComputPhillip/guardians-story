# 4. Characters

Combat kits use the classes, minors and weapons from `stewards_combat_system.md`, and talents come from the workbook's Talents sheet.

## The player: "the Recruit"

A hamlet-born guild Apprentice with no name worth mentioning. The player chooses gender, hamlet of birth (which sets their home hold) and starting combat major (Martial, Marksmanship or Magic). Their level cap is 100 because heroes are the exception.

* **Arc:** a nobody, then a pawn, then a player in the game of holds, then a guardian (or a king).
* **Signature item:** **Rook's Blade**, an Arming Sword (`SWORD`) that grows with the player from tier 1 to Steward-grade (tier 5). Players who don't use a sword carry it as a sigil, and it can be reforged into their main weapon at a Stonepass smithy in Act III.

## Antagonists

| Character | Role | Want | Fear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brother Tamsin** | The hidden villain: a Faith healer and Blight cultist | To wake Drakrevor and "cleanse" a corrupt realm | That he is wrong, and the Blight is only hunger |
| **Sir Robert of First Hearth** | The political rival | The crown | Being remembered as a usurper |
| **Sir John the Coin** | The schemer | To own every road, whoever sits the throne | Poverty: he was born in Sunward's oasis hamlets |
| **Harlan Grey** | The usurper from below | To be a lord, so his men are never hunted again | Dying a nobody |
| **Sir John Ironhand** | Ally or enemy depending on choices | Revenge for Gerrit, and Stonepass free of any crown | His clans breaking without a war to unite them |
| **Drakrevor** | The Cataclysm of the Heart | To feed | Nothing |

## Minor characters

* **Dame Alice of First Hearth:** spymaster, and a possible secret ally if the player exposes Sir Robert's Reaver deal privately.
* **Dame Matilda:** a Court Mage who could be won over. She wants the Blight studied, not worshipped.
* **"Mother" Agna:** the Oxdon guildmaster who sells contracts to everyone. A source of rumours.
* **Vashti Kell:** the Blightwatch guildmaster and Sunward's eyes on the Blighted.
* **Sela Thorn:** the Westwatch guildmaster, who gives the player beast-hunting contracts.
