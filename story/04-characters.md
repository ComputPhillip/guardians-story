# 4. Characters

Combat kits use the classes, minors and weapons from `stewards_combat_system.md`, and talents come from the workbook's Talents sheet.

## The player: "the Recruit"

A hamlet-born guild Apprentice with no name worth mentioning. The player chooses gender, hamlet of birth (which sets their home hold) and starting combat major (Martial, Marksmanship or Magic). Their level cap is 100 because heroes are the exception.

* **Arc:** none written. The player makes their own story through life paths, standing and attitude (see [07-life-paths.md](07-life-paths.md)).
* **Optional item:** **the Warden's Blade** (only for players who meet Walter Underhill on the road), an Arming Sword (`SWORD`) that grows with the player from tier 1 to Steward-grade (tier 5). Players who don't use a sword carry it as a sigil, and it can be reforged into their main weapon at a Stonepass smithy.

## Antagonists

| Character | Role | Want | Fear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brother William** | The hidden villain: a Faith healer and Blight cultist | To wake Drakrevor and "cleanse" a corrupt realm | That he is wrong, and the Blight is only hunger |
| **Sir Robert of First Hearth** | The political rival | The crown | Being remembered as a usurper |
| **Sir John the Coin** | The schemer | To own every road, whoever sits the throne | Poverty: he was born in Sunward's oasis hamlets |
| **John Blackbeard** | The usurper from below | To be a lord, so his men are never hunted again | Dying a nobody |
| **Sir John Ironhand** | Ally or enemy depending on choices | Revenge for John atte Brook, and Stonepass free of any crown | His clans breaking without a war to unite them |
| **Drakrevor** | The Cataclysm of the Heart | To feed | Nothing |

## Minor characters

* **Dame Alice of First Hearth:** spymaster, and a possible secret ally if the player exposes Sir Robert's Reaver deal privately.
* **Dame Matilda:** a Court Mage who could be won over. She wants the Blight studied, not worshipped.
* **Juliana Carter:** the Oxdon guildmaster who sells contracts to everyone. A source of rumours.
* **Beatrice Black:** the Blightwatch guildmaster and Sunward's eyes on the Blighted.
* **Richard Thomson:** the Westwatch guildmaster, who gives the player beast-hunting contracts.
