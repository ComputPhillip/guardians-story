# 6. Side Stories and Recurring Events

## Faction arcs

### The Crows' War (Reedwatch, Acts II–IV)
The Grey Crows (38 men) and the Red Crows (13) feud in the fens. The player can:
* **Back the Red Crows.** They absorb the Grey Crows, become Reedwatch's new garrison and swear to Wenna.
* **Back Harlan Grey.** He becomes Lord of Reedwatch, a commoner lord. This angers every house and inspires every hamlet.
* **Break both.** Reedwatch is left defenceless when the horde comes.

### The Salt Road (Sunward, Acts II–III)
Follow the Blight-salt trade from the Salt Jackals' cache on **Saltcrown Mesa** to Sunward's alchemists and finally to Brother Tamsin. Exposing Casimir Sorel's raids can topple House Sorel, or the player can blackmail Casimir for coin and his outlaws.

### The Iron Reavers' Price (Stonepass, Act II)
The Reavers are Durn deserters paid by Hale. The player can bring them home to Stonepass (Hrothild pardons them if the player vouches), let Hale keep them, or wipe them out.

### The Ash Hounds (Sunward, Act III)
Blight-scarred survivors of a fallen March fort. They know the way into the Broken March. Their chief asks for one thing: to retake their fort. If the player helps, the fort becomes a staging post for Act V.

| **The Deserted Farm** | Frontier | A farmhouse where a family vanished. Their footprints lead toward the barrow. *(A horror-mystery, and the first Blight hint in Act I.)* |

| **The Heart-Born** | Any | A hamlet child is born with *Blight-hardened*, and the villagers want them gone. Protect the child, give them to Tamsin's chapel, or let the village decide. |

## Recurring world events (tie-ins with the living world simulation)

* **Market days.** Each hold's market day (Sunward on Monday, Reedwatch on Tuesday, First Hearth on Wednesday, Canopy Rest on Thursday, Stonepass on Friday) brings rumours, contracts and the chance of a kidnapping or poisoning.
* **Camp moves.** When a band's camp is beaten twice in three weeks, it moves, and the story reports it ("the Iron Reavers have broken camp").
* **Calamity risings.** Each calamity "is named when it rises": the game generates its name, and the rumour mill spreads it across the realm.
* **Caravan losses.** Raids on the roads change prices across the realm. Players who expose who pays the raiders can shift the economy.
* **Hall attacks.** Once the player holds a Hall, rival houses and bands may target it. Its defence and staff come from the player's recruits.
