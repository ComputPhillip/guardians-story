# 6. Side Stories and Recurring Events

## Faction arcs

### The Crows' War (Aquifer Gate)
The Grey Crows (38 men) and the Red Crows (13) feud in the fens. The player can:
* **Back the Red Crows.** They absorb the Grey Crows, become Aquifer Gate's new garrison and swear to Agnes atte Green.
* **Back John Blackbeard.** He becomes Lord of Aquifer Gate, a commoner lord. This angers every house and inspires every hamlet.
* **Break both.** Aquifer Gate is left defenceless when the horde comes.

### The Salt Road (Sol-Spire)
Follow the Blight-salt trade from the Salt Jackals' cache on **Saltcrown Mesa** to Sol-Spire's alchemists and finally to Brother William. Exposing Sir John the Coin's raids can topple Sol-Spire, or the player can blackmail Sir John the Coin for coin and his outlaws.

### The Iron Reavers' Price (Basalt Redoubt)
The Reavers are Basalt Redoubt deserters paid by Sir Robert. The player can bring them home to Basalt Redoubt (Sir John Ironhand pardons them if the player vouches), let Sir Robert keep them, or wipe them out.

### The Ash Hounds (Sol-Spire)
Blight-scarred survivors of a fallen Verge fort. They know the way into the Fracture Verge. Their chief asks for one thing: to retake their fort. If the player helps, the fort becomes a staging post for anyone marching on the Heart.

## Living-world arcs (built on the map's incidents)

### The Last Spring
**Juliana the Warlock** has warded the last spring in the Sol-Spire desert and takes a toll on every load of water. The water-sellers pay her and pass the cost to the oasis hamlets. On day 5 the **Company of the Lantern** duels her and wins. The player can:
* **Side with the Lantern.** The spring is freed, and the price of water and food in Sol-Spire drops.
* **Side with Juliana.** She keeps the spring and owes the player a warlock's favour, which the player can call on later.
* **Broker a deal.** Sir John the Coin buys the spring outright, and the desert pays him instead.

Wherever she falls, Juliana is **exiled to the warlock's refuge** outside First Monolith. Sir Robert visits her there in secret.

### The Red Lances' Hire
**Robert atte Lane** goes keep to keep offering his company. Unpaid, the Lances squeeze Sol-Spire farmsteads. Hire them (it costs coin every week), point them at an outlaw camp, or break them before they turn bandit.

### Margaret's Circle
Follow **Margaret the Sage** from audience to audience (Ribcage Vault, First Monolith, Basalt Redoubt) to the night when all five stone circles meet at once. She is building a council of mages to stand against the Blight without the lords. Joining gives Joan Thomson and other mage companions a stronger arc, but every lord sees an unsworn mage council as a threat.

### Beacon Night
Once a calamity has risen, a surging rift overruns a hamlet near the Verge. The player must ride the **watchtower line**, relighting beacons the Blight has put out before word of the raid reaches the towns by mouth alone. A town that hears in time sends its garrison; one that doesn't loses its hamlets.

## Recurring world events (tie-ins with the living world simulation)

* **Word of mouth.** Everything the player does spreads only as fast as people carry it. A deed in Aquifer Gate is unknown in Basalt Redoubt until a carrier or traveller brings it. Reputation is per town.
* **Town states.** A Starving town draws outlaws and pulls its patrols in; a Thriving one patrols far. Feeding a town (hauling grain, clearing raiders off its cart roads) is a quest in itself.
* **Hamlet falls.** Three raids in three weeks and a hamlet is abandoned. Retaking it and keeping it safe for a fortnight lets townsfolk resettle it.
* **Blight leaks.** Any Necro-Plinth lair left alone for three weeks leaks a pack onto the nearest hamlet. The guilds' work matters, and so does neglecting it.
* **Market days.** Each hold's market day (Sol-Spire on Monday, Aquifer Gate on Tuesday, First Monolith on Wednesday, Ribcage Vault on Thursday, Basalt Redoubt on Friday) brings rumours, contracts and the chance of a kidnapping or poisoning.
* **Camp moves.** When a band's camp is beaten twice in three weeks, it moves, and the story reports it ("the Iron Reavers have broken camp").
* **Calamity risings.** Each calamity "is named when it rises": the game generates its name, and the rumour mill spreads it across the realm.
* **Caravan losses.** Raids on the roads change prices across the realm. Players who expose who pays the raiders can shift the economy.
* **Hall attacks.** Once the player holds a Hall, rival houses and bands may target it. Its defence and staff come from the player's recruits.
