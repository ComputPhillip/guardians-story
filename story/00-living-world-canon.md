# 0. Living World Canon

This file collects what `Stewards_Living_World_Map.html` already simulates and says how the story uses it. **Where the map and the story disagree, the map wins.** Update the story to match it.

## What the map simulates

* **A 91-day run with a clock.** Every named person has a bed, a table, work and company, and follows a daily routine with market days, holy days, bells and evenings at the inn.
* **Word of mouth.** News starts with the people who were there and spreads at shared tables, markets and inns. It reaches another town only when someone carries it. **A town's opinion of the player is what its people have heard.**
* **Town states.** Each town is Starving, Lean, Fed or Thriving, depending on its granary. This sets how far patrols go and whether outlaws come.
* **Hamlets** are raided at 1.5 times their defence and held at 2.5 times. **Three raids in three weeks and a hamlet is abandoned**, and the town takes in its refugees.
* **Guilds** grow from 30 members up to 90 as hamlets fall, and send parties of five out after lairs.
* **Lairs** refill by tier, from vermin in a day to a cataclysm in a month. **A Blighted lair left alone for three weeks leaks** a pack onto the nearest hamlet.
* **The economy.** Real shipments travel the roads, towns post buy orders, and prices move between half and double the base value.

## The Book of Hours names

Map people carry period English names (John, Agnes, Alice, Thomas, with surnames like atte Well and Swift). **Every character in the story is a roster person.** Use [04-characters.md](04-characters.md) to see who plays which part, and when a new part is needed, cast it from the rosters rather than inventing a name. The only exception is **Juliana the Warlock**, who is already on the map.
